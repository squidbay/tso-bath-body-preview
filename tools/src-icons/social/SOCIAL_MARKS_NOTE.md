# Social platform marks

Geometry: Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0) — single-path renditions of each platform's official glyph. Trademarks belong to the platforms.

P3 renders them monochrome (ivory #F1E6CC at rest, gold #D4B46A on hover). Not every platform permits tinted single-color marks; several allow only their brand color or pure black/white. Legal review against each platform's current guidelines is required before launch:
- Instagram: https://about.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/
- Facebook: https://about.meta.com/brand/resources/facebookapp/logo/
- TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/about/brand
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/resources/brand-resources/
- Pinterest: https://business.pinterest.com/brand-guidelines/
- Etsy: https://www.etsy.com/legal/trademark/

If a platform refuses tinted use, swap the footer button to the platform's own color or to white, per its guideline. Do not redraw the glyphs.
